Light Path Series

Everyday formation for the woman in the middle of real life.

The Light Path Series isn't a program. It's a posture — one that says you can walk closely with God even when life is full and hard and beautiful all at once. These resources were built around a single question: what does it look like to stay formed when you don't have hours to spare?

"She opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of kindness is on her tongue."
PROVERBS 31:26

The Light Path Series is designed for women who want to walk with God consistently — not perfectly. Each resource is accessible and practical: a 30-day prayer guide that builds real rhythm, scripture affirmation cards that anchor your identity week by week, and a formation journal that guides you through twelve weeks of honest, unhurried reflection.

These aren't workbooks that demand three hours on a Sunday morning. They're tools that travel with you — through the carpool line, the quiet Tuesday, the season where nothing feels like enough. The Light Path meets you there and points you forward.

Every resource in the series is designed to stand alone or work together. Start with the Prayer Practice Guide and let it open you. Move into the Affirmation Cards and let them hold you. Come back to the Journal when you're ready to go deeper. There's no wrong door in.

Our Story

Built for the woman who keeps showing up.

WORDARO started not with a business plan, but with a gap — the distance between the kind of discipleship resources available and the kind of women who actually needed them. Women in the thick of ordinary life. Women who love God and are still trying to figure it all out.

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path."
PSALM 119:105

WORDARO began with a simple conviction: Christian women deserve resources that take their real lives seriously. Not idealized versions. Not spiritual highlight reels. Just honest, beautiful tools for formation — wherever you are in the season. The name carries the word at the center of everything: the Word. That's what WORDARO is built around.

Every resource we create is shaped by the same belief — that formation doesn't require a degree, a retreat, or a quiet house. It requires a willingness to return. To the Word. To the practice. To the God who meets us in the middle of the mess and calls it holy ground.

We design with care because we believe beauty matters. A well-crafted resource is an act of respect — it says your time and your spirit are worth the investment. We make things that feel as good to hold as they are to use. And we make them for you: the woman showing up one more time, in whatever season this is, choosing to be formed.
